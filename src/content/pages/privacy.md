**Last updated on October 9, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how alwaisy.dev ("we," "us," or "our"), operated by Awais Alwaisy, collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit this website. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to maintaining transparent, minimal data practices across our publication.

## 1. Information We Collect

alwaisy.dev is primarily an editorial publication and portfolio site covering solo product founders. We do not require account registration, and we never sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

- **Analytics Data:** We use Simple Analytics, a privacy-first analytics provider. Simple Analytics does not use cookies, does not store persistent identifiers, and does not track visitors across websites. All aggregated metrics (page views, referrer sources, device categories) are processed without recording personal data or IP addresses, in full compliance with GDPR, PECR, and CCPA standards.
- **Direct Correspondence:** If you reach out via email at [hello@alwaisy.dev](mailto:hello@alwaisy.dev) or through our contact form, we collect the email address and message contents you provide solely to read and respond to your inquiry. We do not add your address to unsolicited marketing lists or share it with external vendors.

## 2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, fingerprinting scripts, or cross-site tracking mechanisms. Any local storage used by the website is strictly functional (such as remembering your selected light or dark mode theme preference).

## 3. Third-Party Services and Infrastructure

Our site is hosted on Cloudflare Workers and Pages infrastructure to deliver fast, secure static assets globally. Cloudflare may process network-level connection data (such as IP addresses and request headers) strictly for DDoS mitigation, security screening, and edge caching purposes.

Our website contains external links to third-party services and social platforms including LinkedIn, GitHub, X (Twitter), and featured products. We are not responsible for the privacy policies, content, or practices of external websites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy notices when visiting them.

## 4. Data Retention and Your Rights

Correspondence data received via email is retained only as long as necessary to answer inquiries or maintain professional records. Under applicable data privacy laws, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal communications you have sent us. To make a request, contact us directly at [hello@alwaisy.dev](mailto:hello@alwaisy.dev).

## 5. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect modifications to our site features or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
